I'm an ISTQB-certified QA Automation Engineer with 3+ years of experience delivering test automation for enterprise web, API, and data-driven applications in Agile/Scrum environments. I translate business requirements into structured test scenarios and work closely with Product Owners, Business Analysts, and developers to define acceptance criteria, validate end-to-end business workflows, and maintain requirement traceability.
